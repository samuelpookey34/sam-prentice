Rate adopted: $1,700 per day. This sits inside the overlap of both bands and below the closest structural comparable by size (Cascade Key, 3,073 sq ft, at $1,723 per night for lodging alone). Every comparable is smaller than the subject property (3,652 sq ft on 2.12 acres), which supports a rate at or above the top of the overlap rather than below it. The rate was set at the midpoint of the band rather than at the top for conservatism. It is also consistent with the $1,714 per day figure discussed internally by Whet Travel and with Mr. Porrata's independent finding of $1,500 to $1,714 per day.
